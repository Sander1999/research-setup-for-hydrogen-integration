# Provenance and licensing

This standalone folder packages Sander Bert da Costa's hypothetical Delft–dunite research model. It preserves the native model, numerical configuration and supporting calculations used in the research proposal. Its reduced source and flow formulation draws on the earlier bachelor/minor models; those projects are not required at runtime.

Open-DARTS is an external dependency, not bundled here. The small macOS build patch is against its recorded source revision; the upstream GPL-3.0 licence text is retained in `build/LICENSE-open-darts`. Consult upstream notices for Open-DARTS and its dependencies. No broad repository licence has been selected for the original model files in this preparation; no additional permission grant is implied by making a GitHub-ready folder.

The two literature CSVs contain small attributed numerical/evidence tables. See their source columns and the accompanying scientific notes. No publisher PDFs, coursework workbooks or private field datasets are redistributed. Generated outputs should be labelled with their scenario settings and numerical limitations.
